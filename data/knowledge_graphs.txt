# Knowledge Graphs

A knowledge graph stores facts as triples made of a subject, a predicate and an object, such as (Neo4j, IS_A, graph database). Entities become nodes and predicates become labelled edges. The phrase gained wide attention in 2012 when Google announced its Google Knowledge Graph to improve search results.

Knowledge graphs support multi-hop reasoning because a query can follow edges from one entity to the next. Building a graph from raw text requires entity extraction, relation extraction and entity resolution. Large language models are now often used to extract triples from text chunks.

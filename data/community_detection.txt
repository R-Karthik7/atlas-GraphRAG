# Community Detection: Louvain and Leiden

Community detection groups the nodes of a graph into clusters that are densely connected internally. The Louvain method, published by Vincent Blondel and colleagues in 2008, greedily optimises modularity.

The Leiden algorithm was introduced in 2019 by Vincent Traag, Ludo Waltman and Nees Jan van Eck at Leiden University. It fixes a weakness of Louvain, which can produce badly connected communities, by guaranteeing that communities are well connected.

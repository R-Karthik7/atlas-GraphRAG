# Transformers, BERT and MiniLM

The Transformer architecture was introduced in the 2017 paper Attention Is All You Need by researchers at Google. It replaced recurrence with self-attention, which lets a model weigh every token in a sequence against every other token.

BERT, short for Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers, was released by Google researchers in 2018. BERT uses only the encoder part of the Transformer and is pre-trained with masked language modelling.

MiniLM was developed by Microsoft Research. It compresses large Transformer models through deep self-attention distillation, producing small models that keep most of the accuracy of their larger teachers.
